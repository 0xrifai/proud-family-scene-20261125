## The scene

> Grandpa turns his hearing aid down for my piano practice and up again for dessert — he says the wrong notes are easier to love when you can't quite hear them, but the pudding deserves the full volume.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes that one short family scene (one or two lines, under 280 characters) to the absolute path `/output/entry.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Requires Node 22.18 or later (TypeScript runs natively). The scene is warm,
lightly funny and specific: a grandad who loves the music and the pudding,
each at its proper volume.
